How it's made

From recovered dust to reliable heat

Every block moves through five steps that keep charcoal dust in productive use instead of going to waste.

The
Loop
01
Recover

Charcoal dust recovered from waste streams in handling, storage & trade

02
Blend

Mixed with clay, spent wash or molasses, and water

03
Mould

Shaped into fire blocks with manual moulding equipment

04
Dry

Fire blocks dried before being supplied to customers

05
Supply

Sold to poultry farmers, households, agri-shops & small businesses
