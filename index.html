Who we are

A Zambian circular economy manufacturer, built from waste.

BioBlend Innovations Limited is a Zambian circular economy and waste-to-value manufacturing enterprise based in Kabulonga, Lusaka.

Established in 2023 and led by Founder & CEO Jonathan Lungu, we transform recovered charcoal dust and locally available inputs into practical, affordable and sustainable products for farmers, households and small businesses.

Mission

To transform locally available waste materials and agricultural by-products into affordable, practical and sustainable solutions.

Vision

To become a leading Zambian circular economy manufacturer supporting agriculture, reducing waste, creating jobs and improving livelihoods.
